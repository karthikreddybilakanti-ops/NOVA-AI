PROJECT SPECIFICATION: Autonomous Fleet Management
1. System Architecture Overview
The autonomous fleet management system coordinates self-driving electric vehicles across urban corridors using low-latency telemetry.
2. Safety Verification & Edge Processing
Edge nodes perform real-time collision avoidance and emergency braking decisions within 5 milliseconds.
3. Fleet Optimization & Charging Infrastructure
Dynamic routing schedules vehicles to fast-charging depots based on battery state-of-charge and peak electricity pricing.
